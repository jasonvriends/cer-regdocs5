16,254 of the documents that are not digital were made by digital tools: pages that were text, degraded on the way to filing.

Census of 269,025 documents by REGDOCS filing date. Orange: the 2024 spike; without it, 2024 reads about 8%.

The share has fallen from 20 to 45 percent a year before 2012 to 5 to 9 percent recently. But it does not fall by itself: in 2024 one filer's publishing system turned 4,137 digital documents into pictures of pages and the rate jumped to 36 percent. And a quarter of all non-digital documents were digital before something degraded them on the way to the CER. Without a rule and a check, a single system can undo years of progress.
