Census of 269,025 documents by the producer recorded in each PDF and by REGDOCS document type.

The cause is the path a file takes, not its subject. Reporting systems and office software produce digital files almost every time; scanners and copiers almost never. The CER's own outputs - orders, rulings, transcripts - are digital. What is not digital is what gets signed: letters and especially affidavits are printed, signed and scanned back. That single habit accounts for nearly half of all non-digital documents.
