The rule: a model may help choose between readings; it must never be the source of a value. The safe fix is a file that already holds the text and the data.

Tests on pages whose text was known exactly: five open vision models on 150 lines, and a September 2026 test on a filing’s contact table. E-mail names withheld.

When a page is only an image, it is tempting to let an AI vision model read it. These models do not leave gaps; they write something plausible. Reading a contact table, one changed a street number, rewrote a postal code, swapped two real e-mail addresses for invented ones and added a recipient who does not exist - and nothing in its output said it was guessing. Even the best of five models we tested got one number in twenty wrong, two wrote text onto blank space, and none could read an engineering-drawing table. In a regulatory record the values that matter - results, limits, names, addresses - are exactly where this happens. So models may vote between readings, never supply the value; and the real fix is filings that already hold the text and the data.
