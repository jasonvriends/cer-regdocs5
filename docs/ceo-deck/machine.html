Search tools and language models read the text inside the file. Where a page is an image, there is no text until OCR guesses. Where a scan carries its own hidden OCR text, that text and an independent reading disagree on two thirds of pages - and an AI answer would quote the hidden text, not what the hearing panel sees. Some files look searchable because a copier stamped a few words on them. And redactions drawn as black boxes leave the words underneath: personal information becomes searchable.
