This ranks everything we learned by value. First, an automatic check at upload, because it turns every other item from advice into a rule while the filer still has the source - and the checker already exists. Second, digital signing, the largest single source of non-digital documents. Third, export instead of scanning, flattening or pasting screenshots - free to do, and one publishing system alone produced over four thousand image-only documents in a year. Then the CER's own fingerprinting of files, data filed as data, proper redaction, text that matches the page, tagged PDFs, bulk access, a signed manifest, and the smaller structural asks. Not worth requiring: rules on page rotation, length, named fonts or table formatting - none of them affected how well a page could be read.
