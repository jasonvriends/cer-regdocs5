What to ask of every electronic filing

Export, don’t scan

PDF saved from the application that made it; never printed, scanned or flattened.

Sign digitally

PAdES digital signatures, including sworn documents, instead of print, sign, scan.

Text that is the page

Paper OCR’d and checked; fonts that decode (PDF/A-2u).

Redact by removing

A tool that deletes the text, and a note of what was withheld and why.

Data as data

Tables as CSV with a data dictionary, maps as GeoPackage, through e-filing.

A check at upload

Automatic, a second a page: “page 47 has no usable text” while the source exists.

Each ask is tied to corpus counts and to the current rule it would change in the filing guidance (docs/filing-guidance.md).

Six asks cover nearly all of it. Export from the authoring application instead of scanning. Sign digitally instead of printing and scanning - the largest single source. Where paper is unavoidable, OCR it and check it, and use fonts that decode. Redact with a tool that removes the text. File tables and maps as data, through e-filing - today e-filing accepts only PDF, and GIS data travels by e-mail or USB. And make it a rule by checking at upload: a sentence in a manual is advice, a check is a rule. Today, image-only PDFs are only 'not recommended' and nothing is checked.
