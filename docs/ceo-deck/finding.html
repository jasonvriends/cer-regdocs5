Census of 269,025 documents, 3,615,229 pages (October 2026). A 5,000-document random survey had estimated 23.2% (95% CI 22.1–24.4%).

Nearly a quarter of everything filed is mostly not digital: the pages are images, or the file carries text that does not match the page. That is 740,000 pages. These are counts, not estimates: every page of every PDF was read. An earlier random survey of 5,000 documents estimated exactly the same rate, so the number is solid.
